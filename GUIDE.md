# SSK's Garden Buddy: the guide

Everything the app does, tab by tab. New here? Start with the
[README](README.md) for downloading and installing.

## Where things are

The panel sits on the right of the game window. **Ctrl+Shift+D** hides it
and brings it back. The ⧉ button at its top (or the **Panel** menu) pops it
out into a window of its own; the same button puts it back. Where it sits
and how wide it is are in Extras, under **Panel**.

- **🔔 Alerts** (Ctrl+Shift+A): volume, voice, quiet hours, test buttons for
  every alert, and a box to add more items.
- **🌦️ Weather**: today's weather, one line per event, plus **a pet team for
  each weather**, **team triggers** (swap teams when the garden reaches a
  goal), and **logging off during weather**.
- **🌻 Garden**: **your garden's worth over time** (a chart for the last day,
  week or month), **your stats** (coins this session and per hour, lifetime
  earnings and harvests, how often each pet ability has triggered), how many
  crops are growing and ready, what the unpicked
  garden is worth, Gold/Rainbow share and **how long until they all are**,
  each **weather mutation**, **crop size** with how long until everything
  is full size, the harvest lock and the open-spot map.
- **💰 Money**: **money in and out** (what you earned, spent and kept over
  the last day, week or month, and whether spending is ahead), **your plan**
  (what you're saving for, how far along, when its shop next opens), **can
  your garden afford this** (each alerted item, yes or no), **where your
  coins go** (by kind, with your room to spend), **your buying patterns**,
  and the **money box** over the game.
- **🐾 Pets**: what your team is doing right now, each pet's strength and
  abilities in plain words, **the best pets you own for any goal**, your saved
  teams side by side, food, and what each pet has earned you.
- **🍀 Luck** (Ctrl+Shift+P): Bad Luck Protection for every egg, plant and
  capsule, with a heads-up when a guarantee is one pull away.
- **🎮 Rooms**: your room's sell bonus, and rooms you've saved with live
  player counts and a Join button.
- **🧰 Extras** (Ctrl+Shift+S): the **seed deleter**, anti-AFK, any other
  scripts, **🛠 Troubleshooting** (tucked away at the bottom: the "Save a
  sample" button and the technical details a bug report may need),
  and **Just for fun**: confetti, a disco with spotlights and a mirror
  ball, a spin, a glitch, and more. They're only on your screen; nothing is
  sent to the game.

Clicking anything in the panel hands the keyboard straight back to the game,
so Space and the other keys keep working in the game while you flip switches.
Typing in a box (a counter, a room code) keeps the keyboard in the panel until
you press Enter or Escape, or click elsewhere.

Closing the game window quits the whole app. If the panel is in its own
window, closing that just hides it; alerts keep playing either way.

## Alerts

**Play alerts on** (in Alerts) sends every alert, voice included, to the
speaker you pick, like the house speaker, while the game stays on your
laptop. If that speaker is off or unplugged, alerts play on the default one
and switch back by themselves when it returns. **Test this speaker** checks
it.

Chimes and the voice have their own volume sliders, so you can have a loud
cash register and a quiet voice, or the other way round. Both are separate
from the game. Mute the game in its own settings and
alerts still come through at whatever level you set here.

Alerts check the official Magic Garden shop feed a few seconds after every
restock. They only tell you; you do the buying.

| Alert | Sounds like |
| --- | --- |
| Moonbinder | Siren, fanfare, announced twice |
| Dawnbinder | Short siren, fanfare, announcement |
| Mythical Egg | Egg wobble, fanfare, and a very excited announcement |
| Amber Egg | Egg wobble, fanfare, and an announcement |
| Dawnbreaker, Emberbloom, Thunderspire | Fanfare and announcement |
| Starweaver | Klaxon and an excited "Starweaver alert!" |
| Windturner, Firepit, XP Potion | Chime and "Windturner alert!" / "Firepit alert!" / "XP Potion alert!" |
| Sunflower | Sunrise chime and a very excited (trying to stay calm) "Sunflower! Stay calm, everyone." |
| Any decor over 500M | Quick ping and "Decor alert!" |
| Ube, Milkcap, Marigold, Legendary Egg | Soft ding and a quick mention |
| A pet turns a crop Gold | Cash register |
| A pet turns a crop Rainbow | Cash register with a sparkle |
| Any pet drops to 10% with none of its food in the trough (same as the game's warning) | Tummy rumble and a heads-up naming foods to add. One alert covers every hungry pet, and never more than one every 15 minutes |
| Thunder | A soft roll of distant thunder, no voice |
| Rain | A rain shower, no voice |
| Dawn | Sunrise chime and "Dawn shop!" |
| Snow | Icy shimmer and "Snow!" |
| Any other weather, including anything added to the game later | Chime and the weather's name |
| Amber | Warm chime and "Amber alert!" |

**Voice** (in Alerts) is one menu with every voice in it. Pick one to see
its details underneath, then **Preview**, **Download** (once; after that it
works offline), or **Use this voice**. ✓ marks the one in use and ⤓ marks
ones not downloaded yet.

- **Characters** (one download for all four; real actors performing
  emotions, and each one talks in character, so every alert comes out a
  little differently):
  - **Poppy**, bubbly and delighted: "Ooh! Oh my gosh! …"
  - **Spike**, grumpy and sarcastic: "Ugh. Fine. … Go on then."
  - **Obadiah**, gloomy and deadpan: "Oh. … Not that it matters."
  - **Prudence**, calm and sensible: "Just so you know, …"
- **Storytellers**: **Storybook**, a warm audiobook narrator ("Once upon a
  time…"), and **Amy**, warm and clear, just the news.
- **Scottish**: **Alba** and **Callum** (press **Try another speaker** to
  hear the other Scottish men).
- **Showtime** (one download for all three): **Stadium announcer**, **Old
  radio**, and **Movie trailer** ("In a world…").
- Or a voice built into Windows, which needs no download.

**Keep speaker awake** (in Alerts, on by default) stops Bluetooth speakers
like an Echo from dozing off between alerts, so they don't announce "Now
playing from…" over your next alert. It sends a signal far too low to hear.

**Snooze** (top of Alerts, or the Alerts menu) silences alerts for 30
minutes or an hour. Alerts still get logged, so nothing is lost.

**Recent alerts** lists everything that fired, including while you were away
or snoozed.


## Weather tab

**Pet team for each weather.** Pick one of the teams you've saved in the
game (Pet Teams) for Rain, Snow, Thunderstorm, Dawn and Amber Moon, and one
for clear skies to go back to afterwards. When the weather changes, the app
switches teams for you, exactly as if you'd tapped that team in the game. It
switches once per change, so if you swap by hand it leaves you alone until
the next change. If nothing is picked for clear skies, the team you had out
before the weather comes back when it ends (unless you've swapped by hand
since). **Apply** next to each team swaps it in right away, which is
a good way to check it works. Every swap is listed in Recent alerts.

**Log off during weather.** Weather can only change your crops while you're
in the game. Tap the weather you want to keep off your garden, and the app
steps out about 30 seconds before it starts. The game window shows a "back
soon" page with a countdown, and (if **Come back when it's over** is on) the
app rejoins by itself about 10 seconds after the weather ends. **Rejoin now**
brings you back early. **Log off now** steps out for a break and never comes
back by itself.

While the app is stepped out, don't play on another device. When it comes
back it takes over from that session, which is the "two sessions fighting"
situation the devs warn about.

Both helpers are off until you switch them on. They're in the modding
policy's "yellow zone" (pet loadouts are the devs' own example of what's OK).
Neither one buys, sells, harvests or plants anything.

## Alerts that ring through quiet hours

Every alert in the Alerts tab has a 🌙 button. Light it up and that alert
rings even during quiet hours and while alerts are snoozed. It's for the
ones worth waking up for, like a Moonbinder at 4am. **Mute alerts** still
silences everything. The computer has to be awake for any alert to ring,
so leave it on (the screen can be off).

## Updates

At the top of the Extras tab. The app checks for a new version when it starts
and every few hours (switch **Check automatically** off if you'd rather not),
and **Check for updates** (or **Help → Check for updates…**) checks right away.
When there's one, a banner appears under the tabs and the card shows what's
new. **Update now** downloads it, checks it's complete and exactly what was
published, then:

- **Windows:** installs it; the app closes and reopens on the new version.
- **Mac:** opens it in Finder. Quit the app, drag SSK's Garden Buddy onto
  Applications, choose Replace, and open it again (right-click → Open the
  first time).

Nothing is ever installed until you press Update now. Settings, counts and
history are kept. Once an update has gone through, the app removes the
installer it downloaded. If there are old installers you downloaded from the
website still in your Downloads folder, the Updates card offers to move them
to the Recycle Bin (Trash on a Mac); it never removes them without asking.

## Harvest lock

For moving pots around without harvesting by accident. Press the 🔓 at the
top of the panel, **Ctrl+Shift+L**, or the switch at the top of the Garden
tab: while the lock is on (🔒, and a small badge in the game's corner),
nothing gets harvested, and "Harvest lock has blocked harvesting" flashes up
if you try. (The game starts its pick-up animation the instant you act, before
anything is sent, so you may see it begin; it's undone straight away.)
Press it again when you're ready to harvest.

**Always protect** (under it) lists the crops in your garden: tap one to
make sure it's never harvested by accident, lock or no lock (your
celestials, say). Tap it again to allow it.

**Never sell pet food** (the switch in the same card). Pet food is
whatever you carry in pots. When you sell, the app locks any crops of those
plants that aren't locked yet (the same lock as right-clicking them in your
bag), then lets the sale go through, and a message says what it kept:
"Kept your pet food: locked Eggplant ×5, sold the rest." If a lock can't be
made, nothing is sold and the message says so. Crops of plants you don't
carry in pots are never in the way.

The lock never sends anything of its own: it points the game's own harvest at
a crop spot that doesn't exist, so the game's server refuses it like any bad
click and nothing is harvested.

## Sharing your room

The Rooms tab's **Open rooms right now** list comes from the community (Arie's
mod). It's often short, because only that mod's users appear on it. The switch
**Share my room on the community list** (Rooms tab, **Room settings**) adds the room you're in to
it, so other players can find and join you. While it's on, about once a minute
the app sends your room code, how many are in the room, your name and your coin
balance to that mod's server; the name and coins also show on that mod's own
site, alongside everyone else who uses it. It never sends your garden,
inventory or anything about the other players in your room. Turn it off and
nothing is sent, and your room drops off the list within a few minutes. The
switch is off unless you turn it on.

## Playing on another device

Logging in on your phone logs the game out here. In the Extras tab, **Come
back after I play elsewhere** (off until you switch it on) makes the app wait
the time you pick (1 to 10 minutes) and then come back, which logs the phone
out. While it waits, a countdown shows on the game screen and in the panel,
with **Come back now** and **Stay away**. If you go back to the phone within
10 minutes of the app coming back, it stops and waits for you instead of the
two taking turns. Finish anything important on the other device before the
countdown ends.

## Seed deleter

In the Extras tab. Pick a seed from your inventory and how many ("all" and
"all but 1" fill it in), then **Throw into the wishing well**. It asks
first, then throws them in one at a time, exactly like doing it by hand,
checking after each one that the game took it. **Stop** halts it straight
away. It can't be undone. Seeds in the Seed Silo are never touched: take
them out in the game first if you want to delete them.

## Copied room codes

Copy a room code or link anywhere (in Discord, say), then come back to the
app: a bar under the tabs offers **Join** or **Save**. The app only looks at
the clipboard when it comes to the front, only reacts to something shaped
like a room code, and keeps nothing else. Switch it off in the Rooms tab
(**Room settings**), or use **📋 Paste** under **Your rooms** instead.

## Team triggers

In the Weather tab, **Swap teams when your garden is ready** holds rules
like: *when 95% of crops are Thunderstruck, only during Thunderstorm,
switch to Big crops*. That's the "bring the Thunderstruck team out for the
storm, and swap it back once the job's done" setup, alongside the weather
teams.

- Pick the goal: a weather mutation (Wet, Chilled, Frozen, Thunderstruck,
  Dawnlit, Amberlit, any hydro, any lunar), Gold, Rainbow, or full size.
- Weather mutations are measured against **all** your crops by default.
  Growing crops catch weather too, just less often than ready ones, so you
  can choose "ready crops" instead if you'd rather the trigger fire sooner.
- Each trigger shows where the garden is right now ("Now 63% (126 of 200)").
- A trigger fires once when its goal is reached, and again only after the
  garden drops back below it (you harvest and replant). If a trigger's goal
  is met, it wins over the weather team, so they don't swap back and forth.

## Backups

At the bottom of the Extras tab: **Save a backup…** writes everything
(settings, weather teams and triggers, egg counts and history, pet counts,
saved rooms, scripts) into one file. **Restore…** puts a backup back, after
showing what's in it; your current settings are saved to the backups folder
first, just in case. The app also saves a backup by itself each day it runs
and keeps the last 7 (**Open the backups folder** to find them). Backups are
a handy way to move everything to another computer, too.

If the settings file ever gets damaged (a crash while it was being saved,
say), the app puts the newest daily backup back by itself when it starts and
tells you in Recent alerts. The damaged file is kept next to it, in case.

## Garden tab

The top line counts your crops three ways: growing (all of them), **ripe**
(the game says they're grown) and **ready to sell** (they meet your rule
from the Money tab: full size, Gold/Rainbow, weather and moon, whichever you
count). "Ready" means the same thing everywhere in the app.

**Your stage** sits under Your garden: where you are in the game (seed
ladder, building your pets, mutation farming) and where your crops are, from
bare ground to ready to sell.

**Gold & Rainbow by your pets** is a line graph of how fast your pets turn
crops Gold and Rainbow: a gold line and a rainbow line, a point for each
hour (12 h, the default, or 24 h), 3 hours (3 days) or 6 hours (7 days),
with a ✨ on your best stretch and a small scale on the left. A break in the
lines is time the game wasn't open. Above it,
the average in crops an hour; under it, the totals, your best stretch, and
how many crops are still plain with how long they'll take at this rate. It
reads the game's own lifetime Gold Granter and Rainbow Granter counts every
few seconds and files what they went up by under the hour. Hours the game
wasn't open here show as a faint line and don't count toward the rate (a
day away isn't dumped into one bar). Two weeks are kept.

**Open spots** also shows itself over the game: while a potted plant or an
empty planter pot is in your hand, a small copy of the map sits in the
bottom-right corner of the game so you can see where the open spots are
while moving plants around. It disappears when you switch to anything else.
The switch under the map turns it off.

The app listens to the same data the game already receives. The only thing
it ever sends to the game is a pet team swap, and only when you've turned on
weather teams or pressed Apply. It only counts pet procs while the app is
open.

Every heading in the panel folds: click it to tuck the cards under it away
(the little arrow turns), click again to open them. The panel remembers
which you've opened and folded. Settings and the long lists start folded
(sound and voice, the three alert lists, harvest lock, weather mutations,
crop size, your stats, room settings, panel, backups, and so on), and a
folded heading shows a one-line summary of what's underneath ("14 of 14
on", "🔊 95% · voice 35%", "sharing off · copy prompts on"), so a tab reads at
a glance even with everything closed. Long explanations show two lines
(the alert lists' sound descriptions one); tap one to read all of it.

## Money tab

The Money tab isn't there at first: it appears by itself after a full day
of coins in and out has been measured, or after two weeks when your garden
and wallet together are worth over 1B (big accounts' numbers take longer to
settle: one 50B purchase skews a day). The Garden tab's worth card says how
far along that is. In the Extras tab, **Money tab** lets you show it now or
keep it hidden instead. Purchases
are counted from the game's own record of what you bought in each shop's
current restock, so they're exact — item, count, wherever you bought it —
from the moment the app is running.

At the top, **Spend or save?** answers the question the rest of the tab
supports: are you growing fast enough to buy the things you really want
when they show up, and how much can you spend on decor meanwhile?

- Your **wants** are a priority list: Moonbinder, Dawnbinder, Starweaver,
  Emberbloom, Dawnbreaker, Mythical Egg, Amber Egg to start with (edit it
  under Your wants). The Amber Egg has its own alert too (Alerts → Shop
  items). Each
  one's next appearance is taken as its average gap from now. The shops
  are random, so this is an average, not a promise.
- **The chart is your money**: the left quarter is the past (up to a week),
  the rest is what's expected, with coins up the side (a y axis). One gold line, **solid for the past week**
  (your wallet plus your ready-to-sell crops, from the app's half-hourly
  readings) and **dashed for what's expected**. The dot is now, with what
  you have; the number at the right end is what you're expected to have by
  the last want's day. **Each purchase is a coral block hanging from the
  line on its day, as tall as its price**, so you can see how big it is
  next to what you'll have then; the two biggest are labelled with the
  want's icon, price and the share of your money it takes ("🌙 −50B · 6%"). **The purple
  line is the final boss**: your money if you bought everything (every
  alert item and every decoration from 100M up, each time it shows up).
  Each line's end amount is written at the right in its colour; a key
  under the chart says which is which (with the ring colours), and **How
  to read this** under it explains the chart in four sentences. If the purple line stays above the bottom you
  could afford it all; if not it dips into red, with "runs out ~date" and
  "short N". The race box under the chart is the long-run version (what
  you make a day against what everything costs a day); the purple line is
  the next couple of weeks with the money you actually have, so it shows
  a squeeze the race can't. Your wants sit
  along the bottom on the days they're due, ringed green (you'll have the
  money), amber (worth skipping for a bigger one) or coral (you'll be
  short); hover one for its price and date, or tap it (or **✏️ Edit what
  you want** under the chart) to jump to Your wants with that want lit up,
  to reorder or remove it. Until the app has some of your past, the left
  quarter just says it fills in as you play.
  With a "Can I buy this?" check, the dashed line shows what happens after
  buying it, and today's forecast stays behind, faint.
- **The buy-everything line** is a race, under the chart. The 🏁 flag is
  what buying every item you have an alert for and every decoration from
  100M up costs a day (every time each shows up, at how often it does:
  the Mini Wizard Tower, Mini Fairy Castle and Wind Turner lead, ~11–14B a
  day each). You (🌱) are what you make a day: this week's earnings once
  the app has them, what your garden is maturing until then. Short of the
  flag it says how far along you are and how much a day is left to go;
  past it, the track turns gold, you become a 🏆 and it says by how much.
  A little ▲ marks your best. The first time you cross it the app
  celebrates (confetti over the game and a cheer) and remembers the day.
  **What "everything" is** lists the biggest costs; decor nobody pings
  (Stone Firepit, Mini Fairy Keep, Stone Torch…) is listed as not counted
  yet until the app has watched its shop for at least 10 restocks.
- The coins line starts from your
  wallet plus the crops ready to sell right now, steps up at the next
  harvest (🌾, when your growing crops will all be ready; they don't count
  before then, and not at all if the timing can't be worked out), grows at
  your recent net-worth pace after that, and steps down when you buy
  something,
  against each want's price on the day it's due (the dots). A dot under
  the line is affordable. Green: covered. Yellow: buying it would leave
  you short for something higher on the list, so skip it. Red: short.
- **Spend up to**, under the big number: what you could spend now, and
  right after each want you'll have bought ("after Dawnbinder 18B · after
  Moonbinder 40B"), if you've spent nothing before then. Nothing while a
  shortfall is still ahead, because spending then only makes it deeper.
- The **estimated value** is what the chart measures your wants against:
  your crops ready to sell now, plus the crops that will have matured by
  then (your maturing rate × time) × what a ready crop is worth, capped at
  the crops your garden holds that can still mature (crops stuck on a step
  nothing will finish, like short of full size with no size-boost pet out,
  are left out, and the Growth card says how many). The rate is what you
  actually do: crops per hour becoming ready, from the app's half-hourly
  records (a harvest never makes it negative). Until it has a few hours of
  those, it uses how fast your pets are turning crops Gold and Rainbow
  (counted, then from their abilities), then the requirement ETAs, then
  your harvest cycles. The Growth card draws it as a plain chart from
  what's ready now to full. A ready
  crop's worth is your ready crops' average (never below the growing
  crops' full-size-and-gold floor). The line rises as crops mature,
  flattens when the garden is full (🌾 full), and follows your usual pace
  after that. The Growth card shows the formula as a picture: estimated
  value when full, a ready / can-mature bar, and where each number came
  from.
- Each want has its own icon everywhere: 🌙 Moonbinder, 🌅 Dawnbinder, ⭐
  Starweaver, 🔥 Emberbloom, 🌄 Dawnbreaker, 🥚 Mythical Egg.
- The **harvest** is predicted from your own history. The app spots each
  harvest in its records (your garden's worth drops while your sold total
  jumps), and from your recent cycles learns how your garden's worth climbs
  and how long a cycle takes. Where you are in this cycle then says what
  this harvest should sell for ("~99B", never less than the full-size-and-
  gold floor, shown as "≥" when that's all it has), and when, if the
  requirements can't say. The Growth card shows what it has learned
  ("3 harvest cycles learned · typical 24 hours · last sold 100B", or
  "Learning your harvest cycle: 1 of 3").
- The signal: 🟢 **Open to spend** (everything covered, with room), 🟡
  **Careful** (covered, but only just, or with a trade-off), 🔴 **Save**
  (short for something you want; it says how much faster you'd need to
  grow). **Safe to spend now** is the most you could spend today without
  losing anything that's covered.
- **Can I buy this?** Type a price (2B, 500m…) or tap a decoration that's in
  the shop right now. The answer: safe, or what it would cost you ("you'd
  have to skip the Starweaver to keep the Moonbinder"), or how much deeper
  it puts a shortfall, and how long your growth takes to put it back. The
  chart draws the what-if as a dashed line.

Below it, five sections, each answered with a picture first:

- **Growth** — is my garden growing? Your **net worth** (wallet plus the
  crops growing in your garden) as a big number, the change this week or
  month, and a chart of garden and wallet stacked over time. A harvest
  moves coins from garden to wallet, so net worth stays level through it,
  and buying a celestial doesn't count as losing money. Under the chart, one
  bar splits what you have into **wallet**, **ready to sell** and **still
  growing**, with what the growing crops will be worth (at least) once
  they're ready and when. **Ready means** chips decide what "ready" is: full
  size, Gold/Rainbow, a weather mutation, a moon mutation (all on to start;
  turn off what you don't wait for). A crop still ripening is never ready.
- **Money engine** — is my income getting stronger? Coins earned per day,
  compared with last week, and bars for each of the last 14 days (the best
  day in gold).
- **Your wants** — the priority list: progress bars (your wallet set aside
  for them in order), when each one's shop next opens, ↑ to reorder, ✕ to
  remove. Below, a chip for each other alerted item, celestials first; tap
  one to add it.
- **This week** — where did it go? One bar split into **invested**
  (celestials, seeds, eggs and pets) and **spent** (decor, tools, the rest),
  with "What you bought" folded underneath.
- **Your stage** — where am I? Two tracks. **Game**: seed ladder,
  building your pets, mutation farming, with one line of advice for your
  step (celestials come first at every stage). **Crops**: where your
  crops are, from bare ground to harvest — 🌰 Planted, 🌿 Grown, 📏 Full
  size, 🌧️ Weather, 🌕 Moon, 🌈 Gold/Rainbow, 🧺 Ready. Each step's ring
  fills with the share of crops that have reached it (with the count);
  "you are here" is the first step fewer than 90% have, your bottleneck,
  and the line under it says what's next and when ("81 crops need a moon
  mutation · Amber Moon in ~3 hours"). Steps you've switched off in Ready
  means are dimmed and skipped.

## Pets tab

- **Your team right now** adds up what the pets that are out do, goal by
  goal: Gold crops, coins, crop size, selling, and so on. Abilities that only
  work in certain weather are listed underneath, greyed out, until that
  weather comes.
- **Pets that are out** shows each pet's strength (and pings you when one
  reaches full strength) (worked out from its size
  and XP, the same way the game does), how long until it's fully grown, and
  what each ability does at that strength ("about 16.6M coins an hour").
- **Team ideas**: pick a goal (Money, Gold crops, Rainbow crops, Gold &
  Rainbow, a team for each kind of weather, Crop size, Faster growing,
  Hatching, Leveling pets) and the app picks the best three pets you own for
  it, says why, where each pet is now (out, inventory, hutch), and what the
  team would do together. Only goals you have pets for are offered. Save a
  team you like in the game's Pet Teams to swap it in with one tap.
- **Best pets for…**: pick a goal (Gold crops, coins, crop size, selling,
  hatch XP, max strength, Gold/Rainbow pets, double hatch, and more) and
  every pet you own is ranked for it,
  including ones in your inventory and hutch, with which saved teams they're
  in. Handy while you build a team.
- **Your saved teams** lists every team from the game with what it would do,
  and an **Apply** button.

Strength is calculated, so there's nothing to type. If one ever looks wrong,
type the right number over it (✎ marks one you set); clear the box to go
back to the calculated one. A "≈" means that species' max size isn't known
yet, so its max strength is an estimate.

The app reads pet, ability and egg numbers from the game's own code when it
loads, so they stay current when the game changes something.

## Luck tab (Bad Luck Protection)

Since the September update, every egg, plant and capsule has its own
counters for its rare results, and the game guarantees each one at twice the
usual number of tries. A Common Egg's Bee by 40 hatches, a Carrot's Gold
crop by 200 Carrots, a Purple Daisy by 500 Daisies, a Dawnbreaker Spore by
400 Dawn Capsules. Rare pets are per egg (a Common Egg's progress does
nothing for a Rare Egg's Turkey), but **Gold and Rainbow pets are one
counter each across every egg**: any hatch that isn't Gold adds to the Gold
counter, whichever egg it was.

- **Primed now** lists everything whose next pull is guaranteed, and you get
  a heads-up (a sparkle and "Lucky! Your next Mythical Egg is a guaranteed
  Capybara") the moment one gets there. The 🌙 on that alert lets it ring
  through quiet hours. The chips choose which kinds get heads-ups.
- Each egg, plant and capsule is one line showing its closest guarantee;
  open it to see every counter. Lines that are close open by themselves.
- The game keeps the real counts to itself, so these are the app's own
  count. Accounts from before the update started halfway, so counters the
  app hasn't seen yet start halfway too (marked ≈). Switch that off if your
  account is new. **Click any number to type in your own.**
- What counts: hatches from the game's log (it catches up on recent ones when
  it starts), crops that start growing while the game is open in the app,
  and capsules from the game's own totals (so those catch up too). Double
  Hatch bonus pets and Gold/Rainbow from pet abilities don't count, per the
  game's rules.
- **Recent hatches** and **By egg** show what you've hatched next to what the
  odds say to expect. **Rare finds** lists each rare result and how many
  pulls it took.

## Rooms tab

Every other player in your room adds 10% to what you get for selling crops,
up to +50% in a full room of 6. The top of the tab shows your room, its
bonus, and a check that the app's player-count lookups are working. The
Garden tab's worth line also shows what you'd get with that bonus and your
pets' sell boosts included, and what it would fetch in a full room (+50%).

**Open rooms right now** lists rooms other players have chosen to share
through Arie's Mod (read from his public room list, no account needed), most
players first, with the names of who's in them. It isn't every room on the
Discord, only the shared ones, but the app checks each one with the game
itself before showing it, so dead rooms never show up. The switch turns the
list off.

Restock countdowns for each shop sit in the Alerts tab under the weather.

**Your rooms** is your own list. Press **Save this room** while you're in a
room you like, **Save** on an open room, or type a room code. Each saved
room shows how many of its 6 seats are taken, the bonus you'd get by joining,
and a **Join** button. Counts refresh every 45 seconds while the tab is open.

## One thing to avoid

Don't run the game somewhere else at the same time: a browser tab, another
laptop on the same account, or another client like MG AFK. The devs have
warned that two sessions fighting over one account can mean progress doesn't
save.

## On a Mac

Settings live in `~/Library/Application Support/SSK's Garden Buddy`. Two
things work differently on a Mac: **Play alerts on** sends chimes and the
natural voices to the speaker you pick, but the built-in Mac voices always
play on the default speaker. And the natural voices are less tested on a Mac:
if one won't download or play, pick a built-in voice instead.

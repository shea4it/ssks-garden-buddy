# SSK's Garden Buddy: the guide

Everything the app does, tab by tab. New here? Start with the
[README](README.md) for downloading and installing.

## Where things are

The panel sits on the right of the game window. **Ctrl+Shift+D** hides it
and brings it back. The ⧉ button at its top (or the **Panel** menu) pops it
out into a window of its own; the same button puts it back. Its width is
in Alerts, under **This panel**.

- **🔔 Alerts** (Ctrl+Shift+A): volume, voice, quiet hours, test buttons for
  every alert, and a box to add more items.
- **🌦️ Weather**: today's weather, one line per event, plus **a pet team for
  each weather**, **team triggers** (swap teams when the garden reaches a
  goal), and **logging off during weather**.
- **🌻 Garden**: **your garden's worth over time** (a chart for the last day,
  week or month), **your stats** (coins this session and per hour, lifetime
  earnings and harvests, how often each pet ability has triggered), how many
  crops are growing and ready, what the unpicked
  garden is worth, Gold/Rainbow share and **how long until they all are**,
  each **weather mutation**, and **crop size** with how long until everything
  is full size.
- **🐾 Pets**: what your team is doing right now, each pet's strength and
  abilities in plain words, **the best pets you own for any goal**, your saved
  teams side by side, food, and what each pet has earned you.
- **🍀 Luck** (Ctrl+Shift+P): Bad Luck Protection for every egg, plant and
  capsule, with a heads-up when a guarantee is one pull away.
- **🎮 Rooms**: your room's sell bonus, and rooms you've saved with live
  player counts and a Join button.
- **🧰 Extras** (Ctrl+Shift+S): the **seed deleter**, anti-AFK, any other
  scripts, **🛠 Troubleshooting** (tucked away at the bottom: the "Save a
  sample" button and the technical details a bug report may need),
  and **Just for fun**: confetti, a disco with spotlights and a mirror
  ball, a spin, a glitch, and more. They're only on your screen; nothing is
  sent to the game.

Clicking anything in the panel hands the keyboard straight back to the game,
so Space and the other keys keep working in the game while you flip switches.
Typing in a box (a counter, a room code) keeps the keyboard in the panel until
you press Enter or Escape, or click elsewhere.

Closing the game window quits the whole app. If the panel is in its own
window, closing that just hides it; alerts keep playing either way.

## Alerts

**Play alerts on** (in Alerts) sends every alert, voice included, to the
speaker you pick, like the house speaker, while the game stays on your
laptop. If that speaker is off or unplugged, alerts play on the default one
and switch back by themselves when it returns. **Test this speaker** checks
it.

Chimes and the voice have their own volume sliders, so you can have a loud
cash register and a quiet voice, or the other way round. Both are separate
from the game. Mute the game in its own settings and
alerts still come through at whatever level you set here.

Alerts check the official Magic Garden shop feed a few seconds after every
restock. They only tell you; you do the buying.

| Alert | Sounds like |
| --- | --- |
| Moonbinder | Siren, fanfare, announced twice |
| Dawnbinder | Short siren, fanfare, announcement |
| Mythical Egg | Egg wobble, fanfare, and a very excited announcement |
| Dawnbreaker, Emberbloom, Thunderspire | Fanfare and announcement |
| Starweaver | Klaxon and an excited "Starweaver alert!" |
| Windturner, Firepit | Chime and "Windturner alert!" / "Firepit alert!" |
| Any decor over 500M | Quick ping and "Decor alert!" |
| Ube, Milkcap, Marigold, Legendary Egg | Soft ding and a quick mention |
| A pet turns a crop Gold | Cash register |
| A pet turns a crop Rainbow | Cash register with a sparkle |
| Any pet drops to 10% with none of its food in the trough (same as the game's warning) | Tummy rumble and a heads-up naming foods to add. One alert covers every hungry pet, and never more than one every 15 minutes |
| Thunder | A soft roll of distant thunder, no voice |
| Rain | A rain shower, no voice |
| Dawn | Sunrise chime and "Dawn shop!" |
| Snow | Icy shimmer and "Snow!" |
| Any other weather, including anything added to the game later | Chime and the weather's name |
| Amber | Warm chime and "Amber alert!" |

**Voice** (in Alerts) is one menu with every voice in it. Pick one to see
its details underneath, then **Preview**, **Download** (once; after that it
works offline), or **Use this voice**. ✓ marks the one in use and ⤓ marks
ones not downloaded yet.

- **Characters** (one download for all four; real actors performing
  emotions, and each one talks in character, so every alert comes out a
  little differently):
  - **Poppy**, bubbly and delighted: "Ooh! Oh my gosh! …"
  - **Spike**, grumpy and sarcastic: "Ugh. Fine. … Go on then."
  - **Obadiah**, gloomy and deadpan: "Oh. … Not that it matters."
  - **Prudence**, calm and sensible: "Just so you know, …"
- **Storytellers**: **Storybook**, a warm audiobook narrator ("Once upon a
  time…"), and **Amy**, warm and clear, just the news.
- **Scottish**: **Alba** and **Callum** (press **Try another speaker** to
  hear the other Scottish men).
- **Showtime** (one download for all three): **Stadium announcer**, **Old
  radio**, and **Movie trailer** ("In a world…").
- Or a voice built into Windows, which needs no download.

**Keep speaker awake** (in Alerts, on by default) stops Bluetooth speakers
like an Echo from dozing off between alerts, so they don't announce "Now
playing from…" over your next alert. It sends a signal far too low to hear.

**Snooze** (top of Alerts, or the Alerts menu) silences alerts for 30
minutes or an hour. Alerts still get logged, so nothing is lost.

**Recent alerts** lists everything that fired, including while you were away
or snoozed.


## Weather tab

**Pet team for each weather.** Pick one of the teams you've saved in the
game (Pet Teams) for Rain, Snow, Thunderstorm, Dawn and Amber Moon, and one
for clear skies to go back to afterwards. When the weather changes, the app
switches teams for you, exactly as if you'd tapped that team in the game. It
switches once per change, so if you swap by hand it leaves you alone until
the next change. **Apply** next to each team swaps it in right away, which is
a good way to check it works. Every swap is listed in Recent alerts.

**Log off during weather.** Weather can only change your crops while you're
in the game. Tap the weather you want to keep off your garden, and the app
steps out about 30 seconds before it starts. The game window shows a "back
soon" page with a countdown, and (if **Come back when it's over** is on) the
app rejoins by itself about 10 seconds after the weather ends. **Rejoin now**
brings you back early. **Log off now** steps out for a break and never comes
back by itself.

While the app is stepped out, don't play on another device. When it comes
back it takes over from that session, which is the "two sessions fighting"
situation the devs warn about.

Both helpers are off until you switch them on. They're in the modding
policy's "yellow zone" (pet loadouts are the devs' own example of what's OK).
Neither one buys, sells, harvests or plants anything.

## Alerts that ring through quiet hours

Every alert in the Alerts tab has a 🌙 button. Light it up and that alert
rings even during quiet hours and while alerts are snoozed. It's for the
ones worth waking up for, like a Moonbinder at 4am. **Mute alerts** still
silences everything. The computer has to be awake for any alert to ring,
so leave it on (the screen can be off).

## Harvest lock

For moving pots around without harvesting by accident. Press the 🔓 at the
top of the panel, **Ctrl+Shift+L**, or the switch at the top of the Garden
tab: while the lock is on (🔒, and a small badge in the game's corner),
nothing gets harvested, and "Harvest lock has blocked harvesting" flashes up
if you try. (The game starts its pick-up animation the instant you act, before
anything is sent, so you may see it begin; it's undone straight away.)
Press it again when you're ready to harvest.

**Always protect** (under it) lists the crops in your garden: tap one to
make sure it's never harvested by accident, lock or no lock (your
celestials, say). Tap it again to allow it.

The lock never sends anything of its own: it points the game's own harvest at
a crop spot that doesn't exist, so the game's server refuses it like any bad
click and nothing is harvested.

## Playing on another device

Logging in on your phone logs the game out here. In the Extras tab, **Come
back after I play elsewhere** (off until you switch it on) makes the app wait
the time you pick (1 to 10 minutes) and then come back, which logs the phone
out. While it waits, a countdown shows on the game screen and in the panel,
with **Come back now** and **Stay away**. If you go back to the phone within
10 minutes of the app coming back, it stops and waits for you instead of the
two taking turns. Finish anything important on the other device before the
countdown ends.

## Seed deleter

In the Extras tab. Pick a seed from your inventory and how many ("all" and
"all but 1" fill it in), then **Throw into the wishing well**. It asks
first, then throws them in one at a time, exactly like doing it by hand,
checking after each one that the game took it. **Stop** halts it straight
away. It can't be undone. Seeds in the Seed Silo are never touched: take
them out in the game first if you want to delete them.

## Copied room codes

Copy a room code or link anywhere (in Discord, say), then come back to the
app: a bar under the tabs offers **Join** or **Save**. The app only looks at
the clipboard when it comes to the front, only reacts to something shaped
like a room code, and keeps nothing else. Switch it off in the Rooms tab, or
use **📋 Paste** there instead.

## Team triggers

In the Weather tab, **Swap teams when your garden is ready** holds rules
like: *when 95% of crops are Thunderstruck, only during Thunderstorm,
switch to Big crops*. That's the "bring the Thunderstruck team out for the
storm, and swap it back once the job's done" setup, alongside the weather
teams.

- Pick the goal: a weather mutation (Wet, Chilled, Frozen, Thunderstruck,
  Dawnlit, Amberlit, any hydro, any lunar), Gold, Rainbow, or full size.
- Weather mutations are measured against **all** your crops by default.
  Growing crops catch weather too, just less often than ready ones, so you
  can choose "ready crops" instead if you'd rather the trigger fire sooner.
- Each trigger shows where the garden is right now ("Now 63% (126 of 200)").
- A trigger fires once when its goal is reached, and again only after the
  garden drops back below it (you harvest and replant). If a trigger's goal
  is met, it wins over the weather team, so they don't swap back and forth.

## Backups

At the bottom of the Alerts tab: **Save a backup…** writes everything
(settings, weather teams and triggers, egg counts and history, pet counts,
saved rooms, scripts) into one file. **Restore…** puts a backup back, after
showing what's in it; your current settings are saved to the backups folder
first, just in case. The app also saves a backup by itself each day it runs
and keeps the last 7 (**Open the backups folder** to find them). Backups are
a handy way to move everything to another computer, too.

## Garden tab

The app listens to the same data the game already receives. The only thing
it ever sends to the game is a pet team swap, and only when you've turned on
weather teams or pressed Apply. It only counts pet procs while the app is
open.

Crops the price list doesn't cover are listed by name under the garden's
worth ("not counted"), so you can report which ones need a price.
Thunderspire fruit is priced as Thunderpeel, and its mushrooms as Stormcap.

If the Garden tab says it can't find your garden, or the numbers stay at zero
when you know a pet just turned something Gold, open **🛠 Troubleshooting** at
the bottom of the Extras tab, press **Save a sample**, and attach the
`garden-sample.json` file it opens to your report. That shows
exactly how the game names things so the detection can be fixed.

## Pets tab

- **Your team right now** adds up what the pets that are out do, goal by
  goal: Gold crops, coins, crop size, selling, and so on. Abilities that only
  work in certain weather are listed underneath, greyed out, until that
  weather comes.
- **Pets that are out** shows each pet's strength (and pings you when one
  reaches full strength) (worked out from its size
  and XP, the same way the game does), how long until it's fully grown, and
  what each ability does at that strength ("about 16.6M coins an hour").
- **Team ideas**: pick a goal (Money, Gold crops, Rainbow crops, Gold &
  Rainbow, a team for each kind of weather, Crop size, Faster growing,
  Hatching, Leveling pets) and the app picks the best three pets you own for
  it, says why, where each pet is now (out, inventory, hutch), and what the
  team would do together. Only goals you have pets for are offered. Save a
  team you like in the game's Pet Teams to swap it in with one tap.
- **Best pets for…**: pick a goal (Gold crops, coins, crop size, selling,
  hatch XP, max strength, Gold/Rainbow pets, double hatch, and more) and
  every pet you own is ranked for it,
  including ones in your inventory and hutch, with which saved teams they're
  in. Handy while you build a team.
- **Your saved teams** lists every team from the game with what it would do,
  and an **Apply** button.

Strength is calculated, so there's nothing to type. If one ever looks wrong,
type the right number over it (✎ marks one you set); clear the box to go
back to the calculated one. A "≈" means that species' max size isn't known
yet, so its max strength is an estimate.

The app reads pet, ability and egg numbers from the game's own code when it
loads, so they stay current when the game changes something.

## Luck tab (Bad Luck Protection)

Since the September update, every egg, plant and capsule has its own
counters for its rare results, and the game guarantees each one at twice the
usual number of tries. A Common Egg's Bee by 40 hatches, a Carrot's Gold
crop by 200 Carrots, a Purple Daisy by 500 Daisies, a Dawnbreaker Spore by
400 Dawn Capsules. Rare pets are per egg (a Common Egg's progress does
nothing for a Rare Egg's Turkey), but **Gold and Rainbow pets are one
counter each across every egg**: any hatch that isn't Gold adds to the Gold
counter, whichever egg it was.

- **Primed now** lists everything whose next pull is guaranteed, and you get
  a heads-up (a sparkle and "Lucky! Your next Mythical Egg is a guaranteed
  Capybara") the moment one gets there. The 🌙 on that alert lets it ring
  through quiet hours. The chips choose which kinds get heads-ups.
- Each egg, plant and capsule is one line showing its closest guarantee;
  open it to see every counter. Lines that are close open by themselves.
- The game keeps the real counts to itself, so these are the app's own
  count. Accounts from before the update started halfway, so counters the
  app hasn't seen yet start halfway too (marked ≈). Switch that off if your
  account is new. **Click any number to type in your own.**
- What counts: hatches from the game's log (it catches up on recent ones when
  it starts), crops that start growing while the game is open in the app,
  and capsules from the game's own totals (so those catch up too). Double
  Hatch bonus pets and Gold/Rainbow from pet abilities don't count, per the
  game's rules.
- **Recent hatches** and **By egg** show what you've hatched next to what the
  odds say to expect. **Rare finds** lists each rare result and how many
  pulls it took.

## Rooms tab

Every other player in your room adds 10% to what you get for selling crops,
up to +50% in a full room of 6. The top of the tab shows your room, its
bonus, and a check that the app's player-count lookups are working. The
Garden tab's worth line also shows what you'd get with that bonus and your
pets' sell boosts included, and what it would fetch in a full room (+50%).

**Open rooms right now** lists rooms other players have chosen to share
through Arie's Mod (read from his public room list, no account needed), most
players first, with the names of who's in them. It isn't every room on the
Discord, only the shared ones, but the app checks each one with the game
itself before showing it, so dead rooms never show up. The switch turns the
list off.

Restock countdowns for each shop sit in the Alerts tab under the weather.

**Your rooms** is your own list. Press **Save this room** while you're in a
room you like, **Save** on an open room, or type a room code. Each saved
room shows how many of its 6 seats are taken, the bonus you'd get by joining,
and a **Join** button. Counts refresh every 45 seconds while the tab is open.

## One thing to avoid

Don't run the game somewhere else at the same time: a browser tab, another
laptop on the same account, or another client like MG AFK. The devs have
warned that two sessions fighting over one account can mean progress doesn't
save.

## On a Mac

Settings live in `~/Library/Application Support/SSK's Garden Buddy`. Two
things work differently on a Mac: **Play alerts on** sends chimes and the
natural voices to the speaker you pick, but the built-in Mac voices always
play on the default speaker. And the natural voices are less tested on a Mac:
if one won't download or play, pick a built-in voice instead.

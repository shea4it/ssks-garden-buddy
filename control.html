Swap pet teams with the weather

When the weather changes, the app switches to the team you pick below, exactly as if you'd tapped that team in the game. It switches once per change, so if you swap by hand afterwards it leaves you alone.

Your saved teams

Teams come from the game's own Pet Teams. Make or edit them in the game and they show up here. Apply swaps one in now, which is a handy way to check it works.

Swap teams when your garden is ready

Swap to another team once the garden reaches a goal. For example: once 95% of your crops are Thunderstruck, swap your Thunderstruck team out for your usual one. Each trigger fires once when the goal is reached, and again after the garden drops back below it (when you harvest and replant).

Playing on another device

Come back after I play elsewhere
Log in on your phone and the game here gets logged out. With this on, it waits a while, then comes back (which logs the phone out).

A countdown shows on the game screen with Come back now and Stay away. If you go back to the other device within 10 minutes of the app coming back, it stops and waits for you instead of taking turns. Finish anything important on the other device before the countdown ends: whatever it's in the middle of when it's logged out may not be saved.

Seed deleter

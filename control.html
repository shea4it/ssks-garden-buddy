Harvesting & ready

What counts as ready

Ripe, and everything you pick here. This is what “ready” means everywhere in the app: the Garden tab, the Money tab and Harvesting unlocked.

Harvesting unlocked ON
Only ready crops can be harvested, so you can sweep the whole garden without looking. While it's on, your lock and protections step aside; they're back as they were when you turn it off. Ctrl+Shift+M.
🐾 Team for harvesting
Goes out when you turn Harvesting unlocked on.
Put my pets back when I turn it off
The pets you had out before come back, unless you've changed them since.
🌱 Quick-plant button on the game
Pick a seed from your bag (▾), then each click plants one in the next open spot. Drag it to move it; put it back.
⏸ Lock harvesting and Always protect are paused while Harvesting unlocked is on; they come back as they were when you turn it off.
Lock harvesting
While it's on, nothing gets harvested. Handy while moving pots around. Ctrl+Shift+L or the 🔒 up top.
Never sell pet food
Pet food is whatever you carry in pots. When you sell, the app locks those crops first so the sale can't take them, then unlocks them again, ready for the trough. Crops you locked yourself stay locked.
Always protect

Tap a crop to protect it: it's never harvested by accident, lock or no lock. Tap again to allow it.

Money box over the game

Show it
Your net worth (coins + garden) and the next weather with a countdown, bottom-right of the game. Ctrl+Shift+B.
How this is counted
  • Net worth is your wallet plus your growing crops. A harvest moves coins from one to the other, so it stays level.
  • Invested is celestials, seeds, eggs and pets. Spent is decor, tools and the rest. "Not seen" was spent where the app couldn't see it.
  • How often things show up is an average, and luck swings around it. Nothing here is sent to the game.

Beta features

🧪 Your stage in the game
Early, Mid or Late above the tabs, with the numbers and sections that matter most at your stage. Still being worked out, so it's off unless you turn it on.

Action bar & keys

⌨️ Action bar on the game
Nine slots on keys 1–9, WoW-style: drag it by its grip. Empty slots leave their key to the game, and keys never fire while you're typing.
Shape
The game's bag slots
The bar has 1–9, so the game's own bag slots get keys of their own: pressing one picks that slot in the game, as its number would.
App shortcuts
Click a key, then press the new one (Esc cancels, Backspace clears). App shortcuts need Ctrl, Alt or Shift, or an F-key.

Second account

Play a second account in a tab above the game. Both stay connected; the one you're not looking at keeps running, muted. The panel follows 🌱 account 1.

Seed deleter

Throws seeds from your inventory into the wishing well, one at a time, exactly like doing it by hand. It can't be undone. Seeds in your Seed Silo are never touched.

Just for fun

Only on your screen: nothing is sent to the game and nobody else sees it. The game keeps working underneath.

Scripts

Switching a script on or off takes effect the next time the game loads.

🛠 Troubleshooting

Details only needed when something's being fixed. When reporting a problem, a sample file helps a lot: press the button and attach the file it saves.

Panel

Panel width
Panel size

Auto fits your screen. In the panel, Ctrl + and Ctrl − change the size and Ctrl+0 goes back to Auto; Ctrl+Shift+D shows or hides the panel.

🎃 Event themes
During game events, like the Haunt Event, the panel dresses up to match. It goes back to normal when the event ends.

Money tab

On its own the Money tab opens after a day of play (two weeks once your garden and wallet pass 1B), showing its progress until then. Show it now skips the wait; Keep it hidden removes the tab.

Backups

Everything in one file: settings, weather teams and triggers, egg counts and history, pet counts, saved rooms, and scripts. The app also saves one by itself each day and keeps the last 7.

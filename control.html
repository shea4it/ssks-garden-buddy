Pet team for each weather

Swap pet teams with the weather

When the weather changes, the app switches to the team you pick below, exactly as if you'd tapped that team in the game. It switches once per change, so if you swap by hand afterwards it leaves you alone. When the weather ends and nothing is picked for clear skies, the team you had out before comes back (unless you've swapped by hand since).

Teams come from the game's Pet Teams. To see them or swap one in now: Pets tab.

Swap teams when your garden is ready

Swap to another team once the garden reaches a goal. For example: once 95% of your crops are Thunderstruck, swap your Thunderstruck team out for your usual one. Each trigger fires once when the goal is reached, and again after the garden drops back below it (when you harvest and replant).

Log off during weather

Step out of the game for some weather

Weather only changes crops while you're in the game. Pick the weather you want to keep off your garden and the app steps out about 30 seconds before it starts.

Come back when it's over

Don't play on another device while the app is stepped out. When it comes back it would take over from that session.

Your net worth (coins + garden) and the next weather with a countdown, bottom-right of the game. Ctrl+Shift+B.
How this is counted
  • Net worth is your wallet plus your growing crops. A harvest moves coins from one to the other, so it stays level.
  • Invested is celestials, seeds, eggs and pets. Spent is decor, tools and the rest. "Not seen" was spent where the app couldn't see it.
  • How often things show up is an average, and luck swings around it. Nothing here is sent to the game.

Playing on another device

Come back after I play elsewhere
Log in on your phone and the game here gets logged out. With this on, it waits a while, then comes back (which logs the phone out).

A countdown shows on the game screen with Come back now and Stay away. If you go back to the other device within 10 minutes of the app coming back, it stops and waits for you instead of taking turns. Finish anything important on the other device before the countdown ends: whatever it's in the middle of when it's logged out may not be saved.

Seed deleter

Throws seeds from your inventory into the wishing well, one at a time, exactly like doing it by hand. It can't be undone. Seeds in your Seed Silo are never touched.

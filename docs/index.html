SSK's Garden Buddy

A friendly helper for Magic Garden. It tells you when something good is in the shop, helps with pet teams, counts your eggs toward Bad Luck Protection, and lots more.

Installing it

  1. Click Download for Windows above. If your browser says the file isn't commonly downloaded, click the ⋯ (or the little arrow) next to it and choose Keep, then Keep anyway. New apps get that until lots of people have downloaded them.
  2. Open the file you downloaded: click it at the bottom or top of your browser, or find it in your Downloads folder.
  3. If a blue box says Windows protected your PC, click More info, then Run anyway. Windows shows that for any app without a paid security certificate. It's safe, and it only asks the first time.
  4. That's it! It installs and opens by itself. Log in to Magic Garden like you normally would, and the helper panel appears on the right.
  1. Click Download for Mac above, then open the file you downloaded (it's in your Downloads folder).
  2. In the window that opens, drag SSK's Garden Buddy onto the Applications folder.
  3. The first time only: open your Applications folder, right-click (or Control-click) SSK's Garden Buddy, choose Open, then click Open again. Macs only open apps from paid Apple developer accounts with a normal double-click. After this first time, it opens normally.
  4. If your Mac says the app is damaged: open the Terminal app, paste this in, press Return, then try step 3 again.
    xattr -cr "/Applications/SSK's Garden Buddy.app" It isn't really damaged: that's just how Macs treat apps downloaded from the internet that aren't from the App Store.
  5. That's it! Log in to Magic Garden like you normally would, and the helper panel appears on the right.

Updating later? The app tells you when there's a new version: just press Update now. Your settings, egg counts and history are kept.

What it does

🔔 Shop alertsA sound and a voice when rare seeds or eggs are in, plus weather and hungry pets. Quiet hours too.
🎭 Fun voicesCheerful Poppy, grumpy Spike, gloomy Obadiah, a movie trailer voice…
🌦️ Weather teamsSwap to the right pet team when the weather changes, or when your garden hits a goal.
🌻 Your gardenWhat it's worth with your bonuses, a chart over time, and open spots when you're moving plants.
🐾 Your petsReal strength, abilities in plain words, and the best team for what you want.
🍀 Bad Luck ProtectionCounters for every egg, plant and capsule, and a heads-up when a guarantee is one away.
🔒 Harvest lockMove pots around without harvesting anything by accident.
🧰 And moreAnti-AFK, a seed deleter, room finder, backups, and a few surprises.

Fair play

No auto-buying, auto-harvesting or auto-selling. It mostly watches and tells you things. The only things it can do in the game are swap to one of your saved pet teams and throw seeds in the wishing well, and only when you set that up or press it. All of its code is open for anyone to read on GitHub, where everything it does is listed.

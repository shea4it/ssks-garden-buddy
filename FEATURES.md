# 🌱 SSK's Garden Buddy: a calm companion for Magic Garden (beta 0.75)

hey! this is my helper app for Magic Garden. it runs the game in its own window with a side panel that quietly keeps an eye on your garden, so you can just enjoy playing. it's a beta, so there'll be bugs. if you find one, tell me (Extras → Troubleshooting → Save a sample helps a ton).

the idea is simple: the game is yours to play. the Buddy watches, gives you a gentle heads-up when something's worth knowing (a restock, weather you don't want, a hungry pet, a team that fits the weather), and catches mistakes before they happen (like harvesting something you meant to keep). it never plays for you: every move in the game is your press. full details are at the bottom.

## ✨ The look

- **a one-minute setup for new players**: about your garden, shop items to watch, how alerts sound, weather to avoid, when a crop is ready, how you sell (and sharing your room), and where the panel goes. each answer is set as you tap it; run it again from Extras
- **🏠 home**: tap the tab you're on again (or the title) for one calm page with what matters now: ready to sell, wallet and max net worth (garden at its best sale price), what needs you, what's coming up (weather, the next spooky night, new quests, the trough) as clock times, a hatch countdown when a rare, Gold or Rainbow pet is close, and your pets out with their time to max strength. every line opens its tab
- **🔒 egg locks**: lock an egg type so it can't be hatched by mistake (hold your Thunder Eggs until the Rainbow guarantee is next too). try to hatch one and the game tells you it's locked, with a button straight to the lock. plus a warning counter at the top of the game when you hatch within 5 of a rare, Gold or Rainbow pet

- **clean and calm, like an Apple app**: soft graphite cards, the Inter typeface, iPhone-style switches, buttons and segmented controls, line icons in the tab bar, and Settings-style tiles in Extras. The overlays on the game match
- **🎃 event themes**: while a game event runs the panel dresses up for it (the Haunt Event: plum and pumpkin orange, a bat flying past now and then, stars at night) and goes back to normal after. It comes back by itself next year. Off in Extras → Panel if you'd rather not

## 💰 Money (the big one)

(every graph in the app reads out its exact numbers when you hover over it)

- **one chart of your money, the Money forecast.** the last week as it happened (solid) and the next couple of weeks as expected (dashed), real dates along the bottom and Today marked, every day the same width. where each line ends is in the margin, and hovering shows the exact amounts on any day
- **celestials that keep coming back** are counted every time they're expected, not just once, so your money line knows about the 4th Dawnbreaker too
- **your wants on the chart.** your wants list (it starts with Moonbinder, Dawnbinder, Starweaver, Emberbloom, Dawnbreaker, Mythical Egg and Amber Egg) sits on the chart, each on the day it's likely to show up, ringed green if you'll have the money, amber if it's worth skipping for a bigger one, red if you'll be short
- **purchases to scale.** every purchase is a coral drop in your money line, as tall as its price, so a 50B Moonbinder looks exactly as big (or small) as it is next to your money
- **the final boss 🏁** is a purple line for "what if I bought *everything*": every alert item and every decoration worth 500M+, each time it shows up. stay above zero and you can afford it all. dip under and it tells you when you'd run out
- **safe to spend now**, with a green / yellow / red "open to spend / careful / save"
- **Money forecast**: your money over the last week and the next couple of weeks on one clean chart with real dates, your wants on the days they show up, and hover for exact amounts
- **"Can I buy this?"**: type any price (or tap something that's in the shop right now) and it tells you what it would cost you, like "you'd have to skip the Dawnbreaker"
- **estimated value when full**: what your garden will be worth once everything's ready, and what it'd fetch with your bonuses or in a full room
- **your wants list**: reorder it, add or remove stuff. tap any want on the chart (or "✏️ edit what you want" under it) to jump straight there
- **money engine**: what you actually earn a day, week vs week
- **money box** over the game (Ctrl+Shift+B): your net worth (coins + garden) and the next weather with a countdown
- the Money tab opens as soon as your garden's been read. it keeps learning your pace over the first day (two weeks for big gardens) and says so with a slim banner until it's settled

## 🔔 Alerts

- sound + voice when the good stuff is in the shop: all the celestials (Moonbinder, Dawnbinder, Dawnbreaker, Emberbloom, Thunderspire, Starweaver), Mythical Egg, Amber Egg, Legendary Egg, **XP Potion** (a must for late game), Wind Turner, Firepit, Ube, Milkcap, Marigold, any decor over 500M, and yes, **Sunflower** (i know. it gets its own very excited, very calm announcement). you can add your own items too
- weather alerts (thunder, rain, dawn, amber moon, snow, anything else), and **night** during the Haunt Event (a soft night chime when the Spooky Shop opens, and whether the Spooky Egg is in)
- pet alerts: a pet turned a crop Gold or Rainbow (cha-ching!), a pet is getting hungry, a pet is fully grown, you hatched a Gold / Rainbow / rare pet, and Bad Luck Protection is one pull away
- seed and egg restock timers, a recent alerts list, snooze for 30 min or an hour, pop-up notifications
- **quiet hours**, with a 🌙 on any alert you want to wake you anyway
- **voices you can actually understand**: Leah, Cori, Alan and Alba, picked (and tuned line by line) for being clear from across the room on a house speaker. the voice is levelled to sit just above the alert sounds, says the item's name first, and pronounces the weird ones right (Emberbloom, Ube…)
- **add, remove and resize alerts in a tap**: type an item (it suggests what's been in the shops and tells you what it'll catch) or pick anything from a dropdown of everything the shops sell, grouped by shop with prices, ✕ to remove, tap any alert to make it Gentle, Callout, Big, Huge or Biggest and see exactly what it'll say
- one volume, sound effects full / soft / off, a slow-and-clear speaking pace, the game goes quiet while an alert speaks, pick your speaker, and keep Bluetooth speakers (like an Echo) from dozing off

## 🌻 Garden

- **five views, one tap apart**: 🌱 Now, ✨ Mutations, 🗺️ Map, 🧺 Harvest and 🏅 Progress, in a row that stays at the top as you scroll, so the tab never turns into one long scroll
- your garden at a glance: how many crops are growing, **ripe** and **ready to sell**, and what it's worth (and what it'd fetch with your room and pet bonuses, or in a full room)
- **your stage**: where you are in the game (seed ladder → building your pets → mutation farming) and where your crops are, from planted to ready
- **Mutations over time**: how much of your garden has each mutation: hydro, lunar and Gold/Rainbow as families, or split out (wet, chilled, frozen, thunder; dawn, amber; gold, rainbow), each its own line, with the next rain, snow, thunder, dawn and amber coming up marked ahead, so you can time the harvest. **leave plants out** (crops you never harvest, like Thunder Celestials) and the shares are of the rest of your garden
- **Gold & Rainbow by your pets**: a gold line and a rainbow line showing how fast your pets are turning crops, hour by hour (12h / 24h / 3 days / 7 days), straight from the game's own counters, with a ✨ on your best stretch and how long until every crop is Gold or Rainbow
- **mutations now** in one card: weather, moon, Gold & Rainbow, and how long until every crop is Gold or Rainbow
- garden worth over time, crop size
- **capture map while an Ostrich or Phoenix is out or ridden**: the crops its Dawn or Amber Capture can turn into capsules, and how many capsules
- **your best sell pets, with a reminder**: each one's share (the Capybara's Double Harvest and Crop Refund included), ✅ when they're all out, or which to put out before you sell and what it's worth
- **action bar & keybinds**: a WoW-style bar on the game, nine slots on 1–9 (plant a potted plant, plant a seed, last team, a saved team, harvesting unlocked / lock), **one press, one action**, every key and app shortcut rebindable
- **quick plant**: a 🌱 button on the game that plants your picked seed (from a ▾ list of your bag's seeds) in the next open spot, one per click; spam it to fill open spots fast
- **swap to your last pet team**: the 🔁 button on the game or Ctrl+Shift+T brings back the team you had out before (press again to swap back). a new team counts the moment you close the pet hutch, put a team out or press the button, never the pets in between, no waiting, no lockout
- **your stage in the game (beta, off unless turned on in Extras → Beta features)**: Early, Mid or Late, worked out from milestones (or picked); the numbers that matter most at that stage above the tabs, and each tab leading with that stage's sections
- **a second account** in a tab above the game: both stay connected (the hidden one keeps running, muted, with your scripts); Ctrl+Tab switches
- **level next** (Pets tab): which pet to level next for the most gain across your account's best abilities (Sell Boost IV over III...), weighted, for the time it takes; three picks that build on each other and a quick win
- **harvesting unlocked** (🧺): a reminder you're harvesting, and only crops that match your rule (Gold/Rainbow, weather, moon, full size) can be harvested, so you can sweep the whole garden without looking; it shows how many match and what they're worth. pick a **team for harvesting** and it goes out when you turn it on, and your pets come back when you turn it off
- **one map over the game that follows what you're doing**: open spots with a pot in hand, the binder map during Amber / Dawn, riding, the Thunderstruck finder, capture pets; tabs on its title switch when more than one fits. drag it by its title, tap to fold, double-click to put it back
- **pets tab in five views**, like the garden tab: now (your team and the pets out), food, teams, rankings, and what your pets made
- **expected burn rate on the money forecast**: after two weeks of watching your money, an orange line shows where you're heading if you keep spending like you have
- **keep a map on the game**: one switch keeps the Garden map's view you pick (spots, thunder, dawn, amber or value) over the game all the time; the maps that pop up for what you're doing still come first
- **fits any screen**: the window, the panel and the maps over the game size themselves to your screen, from a 13" laptop to a 1440p monitor (Panel size: Auto, Small to Extra large; Ctrl + / Ctrl −), and work with touch
- **Thunderstruck finder over the game while a Thunder Wolf is out**: your Thunderstruck crops glowing, how many are left, so the Wolf can turn them Thundercharged
- **binder map over the game during Amber Moons and Dawns**: your binder and its 8 spots, lit crops to move in (pulsing), the other moon's crops to swap out, open binder spots, and the time left, live as you move pots
- **garden map with a to-do.** your garden tile by tile: which plants to **cleanse** (Dawn crops next to a Moonbinder, blocking Amberbound) and which to **pot beside a Moonbinder** (Amberlit crops that would bind to Amberbound there), with where to put them and what each is worth. also a value view, and the open spots view, which pops up over the game while you hold a pot
- **harvest lock** (Ctrl+Shift+L) so you can move pots around without harvesting by accident
- **never sell pet food**: locks your pets' food before you sell so it can't get sold
- always-protect list for crops you never want harvested by accident

## 🐾 Pets

- every pet's real strength (fix it by typing over it if it's off), how long until it's fully grown, and what its abilities actually do in plain words
- **time to max strength** for every pet that's still growing: out and fed on its own, with your team's XP Boost, and with your two best XP Boost pets, plus how much faster your XP pets make everything grow
- **🌙 nocturnal pets** marked (Bat, Platypus, Red Fox, Moth, Snake, Toad: up to +20 STR at night)
- your team right now, all added up (coins an hour, sell bonus, hatch XP…)
- **which eggs to hunt**: where your pets are short on power, the eggs that could fill it, and about what a hatch of each egg is worth to you (judged by the pets of each kind you've had)
- best pets for any goal (coins, Gold, Rainbow, crop size, hatching…), ranked from every pet you own
- **your saved teams** as cards (each pet's picture, strength and Pet Power, the team's total, the abilities it covers) with Put this team out, and a **team builder** to try any three pets
- **team suggestions when your garden is ready**: once your garden hits a goal, like "once 95% of my crops are Thunderstruck", or every crop is full size, or Gold / Rainbow, the app suggests the team you picked. **You press Put out**; it never swaps by itself (you set them up in the Weather tab)
- pet food (who's hungry, what's in the trough) and what your pets have made
- **🏷️ name new pets** (off unless you turn it on): by **hatch number** (the game's eggs-hatched count, so your 3,445th hatch is "3445"; a Double Hatch's extra pet gets the next number) or a random **cute name** from about 15,000 (some fit the species, Gold and Rainbow pets get their own, your best pets by Pet Power get grand ones like "Maximus" or "Empress Tofu", and no repeats across thousands of hatches). plus **name my unnamed pets**: one press gives every pet without a name a cute one. pets that already have a name keep it. rename any pet once in the game first so the app learns how

## 🌦️ Weather

- today's weather
- **🌙 night** during the Haunt Event, to the minute from the game's own clock: when the next night is, or that it's night now, until when, and how much stronger nocturnal pets are right now (up to +20 STR at midnight)
- **pet food that needs weather** (off unless you turn it on): when a weather you'll stay in for starts, a card offers to plant out the pet food in your trough that can't fill a pet's bar without a mutation (one press per pot), and to pot it back up when it ends (one press per plant); you can see and change which kinds
- **a weather planner**: each weather now and next, what it could do to your crops, and your plan for it (the team it'll suggest, or that you'd rather not have it)
- **a suggested team for each weather**: pick a team for rain, snow, thunder, dawn and amber moon (and clear skies). When the weather changes, a card on the game suggests it, with **Put out**: you press it. It never swaps your pets by itself, and the card goes away once that weather is over, so you don't come back to a stale one. for clear skies, **detect my usual team** uses the last team you ran 10+ minutes in clear weather, so you never have to update it
- **weather you don't want**: a **gentle ping** (a soft chime, a note, a desktop notification) when it starts, so it never sneaks up on you; and, if you like, **step away** from it: shortly before it starts, a card asks "Rain is coming: step away?" (**Step away** / **Stay in**), and when it's over a big countdown turns into a sunny **Safe to join!** with Join the game. A few seconds into the weather, a **Reconnect when Rain ends** button floats in: press it, and you're reconnected when that weather ends (for that weather only; never on by default). Otherwise it never steps out or comes back by itself
- **desktop notifications**: weather (step away, it's over) and team suggestions, so you hear about them while you're away from the game

## 🍀 Luck

- **"am i about to pull something rare?"** at the top: your nearest rare pull big (icon, odds like "1 in 50", "4 more Amber Eggs and it's guaranteed", "96 Amber Eggs without a Phoenix so far"), glowing gold when it's your next one, then the other rare pulls that are close. gold-eggplant-type counters stay in the lists
- Bad Luck Protection counters for every egg, plant and capsule, counted automatically: every egg's rare pets and its own Gold and Rainbow pets, as the game counts them. each one starts the first time the app sees that result, so it's the game's real count, never a guess ("waiting for the first one" until then). tracked one yourself? type it in and it counts on from there
- a heads-up when a guarantee is one pull away
- recent hatches, what each egg has given you vs the odds, and your rare finds

## 🎮 Rooms

- your room's sell bonus (+10% per other player, up to +50%), so it's worth being in a full room when you sell
- **open rooms right now**: rooms other players are sharing on Arie's Mod's public room list, with how full each one is (the app checks each one with the game first, so they're real and current). one tap to join (unless it's full)
- **share my room**: the app asks when it starts (with a "don't ask me again"), or flip it on in Rooms → Room settings and the room you're in gets posted to Arie's Mod's room list, so people looking for a full room can find you and hop in. it shows up on Arie's Mod's public rooms page and in everyone's "open rooms right now" in the Buddy. it sends your room code, how many are in it, your name and your coins (at most once a minute), and it stops the second you turn it off
- your saved rooms with how full they are, and join / save by code
- copy a room code anywhere (like Discord) and the app offers to join or save it

## 🤝 Fair play: what it sends

- it **reads** your game's own data in its window, plus the game's public shop feed
- it sends game commands **only when you press something, one press, one action, never on a timer or by itself** (the one thing that waits for a moment is **Reconnect when [weather] ends**, and only after you've pressed it, for that weather; the one exception, off unless you use it, is **pet naming**: name new pets renames each new pet right after your hatch, and name my unnamed pets names every unnamed pet from one press; both change nothing but names): putting out one of your saved teams (Put out, Apply, the 🔁, an action-bar key, turning Harvesting unlocked on or off with a harvest team picked), putting out pets from your hutch (the team builder), planting one seed or potted plant (the action bar, quick plant, the pet-food pots card), potting one plant back up (the pet-food pots card), throwing a seed into the wishing well (seed deleter), and locking a crop kind (only with "never sell pet food" on, when you sell)
- the harvest lock doesn't send anything. it just makes the game's own harvest miss
- the **only** thing that sends anything about you is **Share my room**, and only if you switch it on: your room code, player count, name and coins go to Arie's Mod's room list (at most once a minute)
- no accounts, no tracking. all the code is plain JavaScript you can read
